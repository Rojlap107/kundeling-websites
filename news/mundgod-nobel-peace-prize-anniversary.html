Mundgod Tibetan Settlement Commemorates 36th Anniversary of the Nobel Peace Prize

The Tibetan settlement in Mundgod, Karnataka, India observed the 36th anniversary of the Nobel Peace Prize presented to His Holiness the 14th Dalai Lama. His Eminence Kundeling Tatsak Rinpoche attended the celebrations, which brought together monks, lay members, and officials from across the settlement.

The Nobel Peace Prize was awarded to His Holiness the Dalai Lama on December 10, 1989, in recognition of his non-violent struggle for the liberation of Tibet and his consistent advocacy for peaceful solutions based on tolerance and mutual respect. Each year, Tibetan communities around the world mark this occasion with prayers, cultural performances, and remembrance.

During the commemoration, His Eminence addressed the gathering, reflecting on the enduring message of peace and compassion embodied by His Holiness the Dalai Lama's lifelong work. The event included traditional prayers, offerings, and a cultural programme by students from the local Tibetan schools.
